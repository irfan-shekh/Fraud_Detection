### [00:30 – 01:00] The Technical Innovation: Dual-Architecture & Leak-Free Engineering
> *"Rather than relying blindly on black-box algorithms or post-transaction leakage, our breakthrough came from rigorous architectural separation:
> 1. We designed a **Dual-Architecture Engine**: a **Pre-Transaction Screening Model** for real-time authorization (sub-50ms) using strictly in-flight data, and a **Post-Transaction Detection Model** for post-clearing ledger reconciliation.
> 2. We discovered that **100% of frauds** occurred exclusively in `TRANSFER` and `CASH_OUT` channels.
> 3. In **99.2%** of attacks, fraudsters completely liquidated sender accounts down to **$0.00**, allowing us to engineer zero-leakage pre-authorization intent indicators.
> 
> By eliminating cross-validation preprocessing leakage and optimizing decision thresholds strictly on out-of-fold training data, our model achieved **99.56% Recall with 100.00% Precision** on the untouched test partition."*
